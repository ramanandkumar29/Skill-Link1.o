# Cancellation Policy and Trust Shield

## Cancellation Policy
- **Zero-Penalty Cancellation:** Clients can cancel any active booking before the technician arrives at the service location with **zero penalty fee**.
- **Cancellation Channels:** Cancellations can be triggered directly through your Profile Dashboard or by requesting cancellation through Lexi AI.
- **Refund Policy:** If the visiting fee was pre-paid online via UPI or card, the full refund is processed back to the original payment source immediately.

## Trust Shield & 7-Day Rework Guarantee
- **7-Day Rework Guarantee:** If the same repair issue re-occurs within 7 days of service completion, Skill-Link dispatches a certified technician for a complimentary re-inspection.
- **TrustScore Metrics:** Worker TrustScores (0–100%) are calculated transparently based on:
  1. Identity & Aadhaar verification status (25%)
  2. Average star rating of 4.5+ (25%)
  3. Minimum completed jobs milestone (25%)
  4. On-time arrival record (25%)
- **Verified Badges:**
  - **Legendary:** Top 1% technicians with 150+ completed jobs and 4.9+ rating.
  - **Top Rated:** Highly rated pros with consistent 4.8+ ratings.
  - **Expert:** Specialists with 5+ years of certified industry experience.
  - **Verified:** Background verified and skill-tested active workers.
