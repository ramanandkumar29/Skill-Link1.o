# Frequently Asked Questions and Policies

## General FAQs

### Q1: Is Skill-Link available 24x7?
Emergency Roadside SOS assistance is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Home maintenance services (Plumbers, Electricians, Carpenters) typically operate from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM daily.

### Q2: How are payments handled?
Payments can be made securely via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Credit/Debit cards, Net Banking, or Cash. Payments are escrowed until the client provides the 6-digit completion OTP.

### Q3: What if the technician doesn't arrive on time?
If a booked technician is delayed beyond the estimated arrival window, you can cancel the request with zero penalty and re-assign the job to another nearby pro instantly through Lexi or your dashboard.

### Q4: Can I hire workers for multiple days or large projects?
Yes, you can book masons, painters, and carpenters on a per-day or custom estimate contract after the initial doorstep inspection.

### Q5: What is the Sahayak AI Voice Assistant?
Sahayak AI is Skill-Link's multilingual voice tool designed to help local tradespeople and non-technical users register and interact using Hindi or English voice commands without manual typing.
