# Services and Transparent Pricing

- **Electrical:** Short circuit fixes, MCB tripping, house wiring, ceiling fans, switchboards.
- **AC & Cooling:** Deep jet pump servicing, gas leakage detection, compressor PCB diagnostics.
- **Appliance Repair:** Refrigerator cooling issues, washing machine drainage, microwave, television repair.

- **Salon & Grooming:** Doorstep bridal makeup, spa therapy, hair styling, skincare.
- **Deep Cleaning:** Full home, sofa, kitchen, bathroom sanitization.
- **Vehicle Assistance:** Car and bike mechanics, puncture fixes, battery jumpstart, towing, emergency fuel delivery.

## Visiting & Diagnosis Fee Structure
- **Plumbers, Electricians, Carpenters, Painters:** Visiting fee is ₹149.
- **Car Mechanics, AC Technicians, Masons, CCTV Technicians:** Visiting fee is ₹199.
- **Deep Cleaning:** Base inspection starting from ₹299.
- The visiting fee covers doorstep arrival, physical inspection, and diagnostic assessment.

## Labor Charges & Diagnosis
- Labor costs are estimated transparently according to standard rate cards (e.g. ₹200–₹800 for plumbing, ₹200–₹700 for electrical).
- Exact final labor charges are confirmed after in-person physical inspection before any repair work commences.
- Parts and materials are charged transparently at actual market costs.
