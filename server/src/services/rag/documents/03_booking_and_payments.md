# Booking Process and Payments

## How to Book a Worker on Skill-Link
1. **Discover & Select:** Browse workers on the marketplace or describe your problem to Lexi AI (in English, Hindi, or Hinglish).
2. **Review Profile:** Inspect the worker's rating (1–5 stars), TrustScore (e.g. 98%), verified badge, distance, and experience.
3. **Confirm Inspection:** Enter your service location and contact phone number. Pay the base visiting fee (₹149–₹199) online via UPI or choose Cash on Arrival.
4. **Technician Dispatch:** The assigned verified technician receives the booking and arrives at your doorstep.
5. **Job Completion & Photo Proof:** After repair, the worker uploads completion photo proof and generates the final bill.

## Escrow OTP Security & Payment Release
- Every booking generates a 6-digit Escrow OTP.
- The client only provides this OTP to the technician once the repair is completed to their total satisfaction.
- This protects customer payments from unauthorized charges.
- Payment Methods Supported: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, Cash.
