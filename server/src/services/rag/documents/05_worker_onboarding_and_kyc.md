# Worker Onboarding and Profile Creation

## How Workers Register and Create Profiles
1. **Sahayak Voice Onboarding:**
   Workers who prefer not to type can use the **Sahayak Voice Registration** wizard. Sahayak asks structured questions in Hindi/English (Name, Trade Occupation, Years of Experience, Operating Location) and records a 10-second voice intro bio.
2. **Standard Form Registration:**
   Workers can also sign up via the Register page by providing their full name, mobile phone number, trade category, hourly rates, and skills checklist.
3. **Audio Snippet Bio:**
   Pros record a short audio greeting (e.g. *"Namaste! Main Vikram hoon, Master Plumber with 6 years experience"*). Clients can listen to this authentic voice preview on the worker's card.
4. **KYC Verification Pipeline:**
   - **Step 1: Aadhaar & Government ID Verification**
   - **Step 2: Skill & Trade Qualification Check**
   - **Step 3: Background & Safety Clearance**
   - Once verified, the profile is marked as **Verified** and becomes eligible to receive incoming customer leads and emergency dispatch calls.

## Worker Dashboard & Profile Management
- Pros can manage their availability toggle (Online / Offline), view booking history, generate bills, and upload job completion photos directly from their account dashboard.
