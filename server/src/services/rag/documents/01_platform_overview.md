# Skill-Link Platform Overview

## What is Skill-Link?
Skill-Link is an intelligent, AI-powered dual-mode hyperlocal marketplace that connects clients with verified local skilled workers and mechanics based on natural language problem descriptions, transparent pricing, and instant dispatch.

## Dual-Mode Architecture
Skill-Link operates in two primary modes:
1. **Home Service Mode:**
   On-demand home maintenance and repairs across multiple categories including Plumbers, Electricians, AC Technicians, Masons, Appliance Repair (Fridge, Washing Machine, Microwave, TV), Deep Cleaning, Salon & Grooming, Carpenters, Painters, CCTV, and Wi-Fi technicians.
2. **On-Road Emergency SOS Mode:**
   15-Minute priority roadside dispatch for stranded commuters facing tyre punctures, dead batteries (jumpstart), fuel runout, engine breakdown, or towing needs.

## Core Pillars & Values
- **Hyperlocal Precision:** Workers and mechanics are matched based on live GPS location, proximity distance, trade skills, and availability.
- **Trust & Verification:** Mandatory Aadhaar identity verification, background checks, and real job completion histories.
- **Transparent Pricing:** Standardized base visiting fee starting at ₹149, transparent rate cards with no hidden fees, and price-lock guarantees for emergency services.
- **Multilingual Accessibility:** Natural voice and text interactions in English, Hindi, and Hinglish.
