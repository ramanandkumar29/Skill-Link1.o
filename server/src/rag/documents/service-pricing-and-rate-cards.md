# Skill-Link Standard Rate Cards & Transparent Pricing

Skill-Link ensures zero hidden fees, upfront rate cards, and transparent labor estimates.

## 1. Visiting & Diagnosis Fees
* **General Home Trades (Plumber, Electrician, Carpenter)**: ₹149 (Includes doorstep inspection & diagnosis)
* **Appliances (AC, Fridge, Washing Machine, Geyser, RO)**: ₹149–₹199
* **Automotive (Car Mechanic, Bike Mechanic)**: ₹149–₹199
* **Emergency Roadside Assistance & SOS**: ₹249

## 2. Typical Labor Charge Guidelines
* **Tap / Valve Replacement**: ₹99–₹199 + parts
* **Switch / Socket / Fan Installation**: ₹79–₹149
* **AC Jet Pump Deep Cleaning**: ₹399–₹499 per unit
* **AC Gas Top-Up (R32 / R410A)**: ₹1,200–₹1,800 (includes leak test)
* **RO Full Filter & Membrane Replacement**: ₹1,200–₹1,800
* **Car Brake Pad Replacement (Front Pair)**: ₹399 labor + OEM parts
* **Bike Engine Oil Change & Tuning**: ₹199–₹299 labor

## 3. Transparency Rules
* Visiting fee is adjusted against final invoice if repair work is accepted.
* Any required spare parts are quoted to client and approved before installation.
