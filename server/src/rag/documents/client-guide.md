# Skill-Link Client Guide

A complete guide for homeowners, vehicle owners, and clients using Skill-Link.

## Finding and Booking Services
1. **Chatting with Lexi**: You can ask general maintenance questions, request advice, or ask Lexi to find verified professionals.
2. **Setting Your Location**: Share your current area or GPS (e.g. "Sector 17, Chandigarh", "Noida Sector 62", "Saket, Delhi") to discover available technicians nearby.
3. **Comparing Workers**: Review worker experience badges, verified client reviews, past job counts, and star ratings before making a choice.
4. **Emergency Roadside & Home SOS**: For critical emergencies, use the Roadside Breakdown SOS or Plumbing Emergency trigger for expedited dispatch within 15–20 minutes.

## Payment & Safety Guarantees
* **Standard Visiting Fee**: ₹149 to ₹199 covering inspection, physical diagnosis, and doorstep arrival.
* **Payment Methods**: Pay securely via UPI, QR code, Net Banking, or Cash after service inspection.
* **Skill-Link Trust Shield**: Free rework warranty if an issue reoccurs within 7 days of verified service completion.
* **Cancellation Policy**: Zero-penalty free cancellation before the worker is en route.
