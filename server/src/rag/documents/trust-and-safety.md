# Trust, Safety and Verification on Skill-Link

Skill-Link prioritizes the safety, security, and trust of both clients and skilled trade workers.

## 1. 100% Verified Professionals
Every technician and trade specialist undergoes strict multi-point verification:
* **Government Identity Verification**: Aadhaar ID and Driving License verification.
* **Criminal Background Clearance**: Police record check before activation.
* **Trade Skill Certification**: Practical trade assessment and experience validation.
* **Continuous Quality Audits**: Professionals maintaining ratings below 4.2★ undergo retraining or deactivation.

## 2. Skill-Link Trust Shield & 7-Day Rework Warranty
* Every verified booking is covered under the Skill-Link Trust Shield.
* If a repaired issue reoccurs within **7 days** of service completion, a certified technician is dispatched to re-inspect free of charge.
* Property damage protection guarantee up to ₹10,000 for covered accidental damages during service.

## 3. Secure & Transparent Transactions
* **No Hidden Fees**: Transparent rate cards with fixed diagnosis fees (₹149–₹199).
* **Safe Payment Channels**: Encrypted UPI QR, Net Banking, Card, and Cash on Delivery.
* **Escrow-Style Protection**: Clients only approve final payments after physical inspection and satisfaction.

## 4. Emergency SOS & Helpline Network
* Priority emergency protocol with 15–20 min dispatch for dangerous electrical sparks, pipe bursts, or highway car breakdowns.
* Instant 24/7 direct dial to verified national emergency numbers (112, 1033 NHAI SOS, 1906 Gas Leak).
