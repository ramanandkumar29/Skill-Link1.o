# About Skill-Link

Skill-Link is an AI-powered next-generation service marketplace engineered to bridge the gap between everyday clients and verified local skilled workers.

## Mission & Purpose
Traditional marketplaces force users to navigate complicated filter menus, search forms, and inconsistent pricing. Skill-Link eliminates friction by allowing clients to simply describe their problem in natural language (English, Hindi, or Hinglish). The AI assistant, **Lexi**, instantly understands the intent, identifies the necessary trade, checks local availability, provides transparent price estimates, and manages the booking lifecycle.

## Core Pillars
1. **Natural AI Conversational Experience**: Talk or chat naturally without filling tedious search forms.
2. **100% Verified Trade Professionals**: All plumbers, electricians, mechanics, and technicians undergo Aadhaar ID checks and trade skill verification.
3. **Transparent Upfront Pricing**: Fixed visiting diagnosis fees and standard labor rate cards with zero hidden costs.
4. **24/7 Roadside & Home Emergency SOS**: Instant dispatch within 15–20 minutes for vehicle breakdowns, pipe bursts, and electrical hazards.
5. **Client-First Safety**: No automatic booking without explicit client approval and transparent review.
