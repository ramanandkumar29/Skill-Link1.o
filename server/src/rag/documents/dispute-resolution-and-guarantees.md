# Dispute Resolution, Guarantees & Trust Shield

## 1. 7-Day Rework Guarantee
* All verified repairs completed through Skill-Link are covered by our 7-Day Rework Guarantee.
* If the identical issue reoccurs within 7 days, a senior verified technician is dispatched to re-inspect and fix it with zero additional visit charges.

## 2. Cancellation Policy
* **Free Cancellation**: Clients may cancel any booking before the technician arrives at the location with zero cancellation penalty.
* **Rescheduling**: Flexible date/time rescheduling available via Lexi or the client dashboard.

## 3. Escrow & Secure Settlements
* Clients inspect the completed repair physically with the technician before releasing OTP or digital payment.
* Payment options: UPI QR, Net Banking, Credit/Debit Cards, Cash on Delivery.
