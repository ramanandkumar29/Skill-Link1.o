# Skill-Link Booking Engine & Lifecycle

Skill-Link ensures a completely secure, transparent booking workflow with zero unwanted bookings.

## Anti-Auto-Booking Principle
Simply describing a problem (e.g. "My pipe is leaking" or "My car stopped") does **NOT** trigger an automatic booking. Lexi will provide troubleshooting or safety steps and politely ask if you would like to find and review a technician.

## Transparent Price Calculation
Each booking generates a clear estimate before payment:
* **Visiting & Diagnosis Fee**: Covers travel, inspection, and on-spot diagnosis (₹149–₹199).
* **Estimated Labor Fee**: Standardized rate for typical repairs (e.g. ₹250 for tap replacement).
* **Platform Trust Fee**: ₹20 guaranteeing safety verification and 7-day rework warranty.
* **Emergency Multiplier**: 1.15x for expedited immediate night or highway dispatch.

## Booking States
1. **Pending**: Request received, searching local matching workers.
2. **Confirmed**: Worker assigned, visiting fee locked, technician en route.
3. **In-Progress**: Worker arrived at location, diagnosis and service underway.
4. **Completed**: Service verified, final payment settled, rating submitted.
5. **Cancelled**: Booking cancelled cleanly with zero penalty fee.
