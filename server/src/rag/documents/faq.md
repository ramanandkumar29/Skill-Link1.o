# Skill-Link Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. What is Skill-Link?
Skill-Link is an AI-powered service marketplace that connects homeowners and drivers with verified skilled trade workers using natural conversation instead of complicated category menus.

## 2. Is there a charge to search or chat with Lexi?
No, chatting with Lexi, getting maintenance advice, and discovering workers is 100% free. You only pay when you confirm a worker booking.

## 3. How much does a visiting inspection cost?
Visiting and diagnosis fees range between ₹149 and ₹199 depending on the trade (e.g. ₹149 for Plumber/Electrician, ₹199 for Car Mechanic/AC Technician).

## 4. Can I cancel a booking if my plans change?
Yes, you can cancel any booking before the technician arrives at zero penalty.

## 5. What if the repair has issues after the technician leaves?
Skill-Link provides a 7-day Rework Guarantee for all verified platform bookings. If the same issue reoccurs within 7 days, a technician will re-inspect free of charge.

## 6. How are workers verified?
Every worker undergoes government Aadhaar ID verification, background checks, and trade skill assessment.
