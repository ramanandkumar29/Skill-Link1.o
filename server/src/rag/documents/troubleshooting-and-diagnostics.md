# Home & Vehicle Technical Troubleshooting Guide

This guide helps Lexi understand root causes of trade issues, safety measures, and preliminary guidance before technician arrival.

## 1. Automotive & Two-Wheelers
* **Engine Not Cranking**: Weak battery voltage (<12.2V), corroded terminal posts, starter relay solenoid failure, or faulty neutral switch.
* **Overheating on Highway**: Coolant low, radiator fan fuse blown, thermostat stuck closed, radiator choked with road dust.
* **Safety First**: Pull car safely to road shoulder, turn hazard lights ON, leave engine OFF for 20 mins before inspecting.

## 2. Electrical Systems
* **MCB Frequent Tripping**: Overload on single phase, neutral loop fault, short circuit in heating appliances (iron, geyser, induction).
* **Switchboard Sparks**: Carbon accumulation, loose wire screw terminals, moisture ingress.
* **Safety First**: Turn OFF main MCB immediately before touching any switch plate.

## 3. Plumbing & Drainage
* **Continuous Cistern/Flush Leak**: Damaged syphon diaphragm or inlet ballcock valve stuck.
* **Slow Draining Sinks**: Hair, soap scum, and grease sludge in P-trap.
* **Safety First**: Turn overhead tank stopcock OFF when pipe burst or flooding occurs.

## 4. HVAC & Appliances
* **AC Blowing Warm Air**: Choked air filters, capacitor fail on compressor (45–50 MFD), refrigerant gas leak.
* **Refrigerator Defrost Failure**: Defrost bimetal thermostat or timer fault causing ice buildup on evaporator coils.
* **Washing Machine Spin Issue**: Unbalanced load sensor trigger, drive belt slip, drain pump impellor blocked with coins.
* **RO Water Purifier Low Flow**: Sediment pre-filter blocked with silt (>100 NTU), RO membrane fouled, pump pressure <60 PSI.
