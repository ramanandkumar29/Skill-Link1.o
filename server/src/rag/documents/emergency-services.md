# Emergency Services & Safety SOS

Skill-Link features a dedicated 24/7 rapid emergency dispatch protocol for critical roadside and home breakdowns.

## Supported Emergency Categories
1. **Roadside Breakdown SOS**: Stalled vehicle on highway, empty fuel, overheating engine, or tyre puncture.
2. **Plumbing Emergency**: Main pipeline burst, severe bathroom flooding, water overflow.
3. **Electrical Hazard**: Short circuit sparks, burning smell from switchboards, MCB failure.
4. **Emergency Lockout**: Lost keys or jammed lockouts for home doors or vehicles.
5. **LPG Gas Leak Guidance**: Safe emergency procedure with immediate regulator safety instructions.

## Rapid Dispatch Protocol
* Priority dispatch within 15–20 minutes ETA.
* Automated emergency checklist shared with the client while waiting (e.g. turn off main water valve, switch off MCB, move vehicle safely to the road shoulder).

## 24/7 Verified National Helplines
* **National Emergency**: 112
* **National Highway Authority (NHAI SOS)**: 1033
* **Fire Brigade**: 101
* **Ambulance**: 108
* **LPG Gas Leakage Helpline**: 1906
* **Women Helpline**: 1091
