# How Skill-Link Works

Skill-Link transforms service booking into a smooth 5-step conversational journey.

## Step 1: Natural Problem Description
The user simply speaks or types what is wrong:
* *"Meri car start nahi ho rahi highway par"*
* *"I need an electrician to fix a short circuit"*
* *"Bathroom ka tap leak kar raha hai"*

## Step 2: Intelligent Intent & Trade Classification
Lexi AI processes the utterance:
* Classifies the service category (Plumber, Electrician, Mechanic, AC Specialist, Cleaning, etc.).
* Gauges urgency (Normal, High, Emergency SOS).
* Detects missing parameters (like current location or preferred appointment time).

## Step 3: Worker Ranking & Matching Engine
Skill-Link queries available nearby professionals:
* Calculates match score based on proximity (GPS/locality), customer ratings (e.g. 4.8★+), job completion history, and trust scores.
* Verifies real-time online status and instant ETA.

## Step 4: Transparent Estimate & Confirmation
The client reviews:
* Selected worker profile, photos, and ratings.
* Itemized fee breakdown (Visiting & Diagnosis Fee + Base Labor Fee + Platform Guarantee).
* Preferred payment method (UPI QR, Cash on Delivery, Credit/Debit Card).

## Step 5: Service Completion & Verified Feedback
* The technician arrives within the estimated ETA (typically 15–20 minutes for SOS).
* After the job is completed safely, the user rates and reviews the professional, updating their verified reputation.
