# Skill-Link Worker Guide

A comprehensive onboarding and operational handbook for verified trade professionals.

## How Workers Benefit
* **Direct Client Requests**: Receive high-intent local service leads matched directly to your trade skills and location.
* **Guaranteed Earnings**: Transparent upfront visiting fee payouts directly credited via UPI with fast settlement.
* **Digital Reputation & Trust Score**: Build a certified digital profile showcasing your years of experience, skill certifications, photos of past work, and genuine verified reviews.
* **Flexible Availability**: Toggle between "Online" (available for immediate job requests) and "Offline" with a single tap.

## Onboarding Requirements
1. Government ID verification (Aadhaar / Driving License).
2. Police background verification clear check.
3. Minimum 2+ years verified field experience in chosen category (e.g. Plumbing, Automotive Repair, HVAC, Electrical).
4. Smartphone with active GPS for route navigation and client communication.
