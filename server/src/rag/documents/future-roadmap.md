# Skill-Link Future Vision & Roadmap

Skill-Link's long-term roadmap aims to build a fully autonomous, intelligent home and vehicle service ecosystem.

## Planned & Upcoming Capabilities
1. **Real-Time GPS Worker Tracking**: Live live-map tracking of assigned technicians with turn-by-turn ETA.
2. **Autonomous Predictive Maintenance**: IoT device integration to predict AC gas leaks or car battery failure before breakdowns occur.
3. **Multilingual Lexi Voice Engine**: Ultra-low latency spoken voice in Hindi, Hinglish, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, and Bengali.
4. **AI-Powered Photo Diagnostics**: Clients upload a photo of a leaking pipe or damaged circuit board, and Lexi diagnoses the exact spare part needed.
5. **Smart Escrow Payments**: Automated milestone payments released only upon client digital signature verification.
6. **Pan-India Regional Expansion**: Scaling verified technician networks to Tier-2 and Tier-3 smart cities across India.

*Note: These capabilities are part of Skill-Link's future vision and are introduced progressively.*
